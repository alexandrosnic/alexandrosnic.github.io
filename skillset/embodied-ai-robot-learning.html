Embodied AI & Robot Learning

Learning from egocentric human data: I turn head-mounted recordings of people working into robot-ready data: visual SLAM for the camera trajectory and a 3D map, hand paths in room coordinates, gripper-ready actions and hand-object contact events, packaged in LeRobot format.

Real-to-sim: I replay recorded human episodes on a simulated arm with MoveIt to check, per episode, whether a robot could reproduce the motion.

Physical AI loops: I combine VLMs, LLM planners and physics simulators (MuJoCo, Isaac Sim) so models can observe robot behaviour, explain failures and correct it, and I work with VLA policies and world models for robot learning.
